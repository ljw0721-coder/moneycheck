When is Election Day 2026? Tuesday, November 3 — what is on the ballot

Views 0

Answer first: Election Day 2026 in the United States is Tuesday, November 3, 2026. It is the midterm election: all 435 seats in the House of Representatives and one-third of the Senate are up for election, along with numerous governor and state races. That is four days from today.

Key points

  • Date: Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (U.S. general elections fall on the Tuesday after the first Monday in November)
  • House: all 435 seats up for election (two-year terms)
  • Senate: one-third of the 100 seats contested
  • Also on the ballot: many governor, state legislature and local races
  • Stakes: control of Congress for the final two years of President Trump's term

Why it is trending

Selection basis: "when is election day 2026" was the #1 trending query on Google's U.S. trending searches feed on October 30, 2026, as voters look up the date and logistics in the final days before the midterms. AP's 2026 election calendar is also being widely consulted for early-voting and certification dates.

Timeline

  • Now: early and mail-in voting under way in most states (rules vary by state)
  • Nov. 3, 2026: Election Day — polls close state by state, results reported through the night
  • Following weeks: state certification deadlines; the new Congress convenes in January 2027

Confirmed / not confirmed

Confirmed: The date (Nov. 3, 2026) and what is on the ballot — all 435 House seats, one-third of the Senate (usa.gov); the midterms will shape the last two years of the current presidential term (AP).

Not confirmed: Any outcome. Polling and forecasts are not results, and no race has been called.

Useful to know

  • Registration deadlines, early-voting windows and ID rules differ by state — check your state election office via usa.gov's midterm elections page.
  • If you have a mail ballot, check your state's return deadline; some states count only ballots received by Election Day.
