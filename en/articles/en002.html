Who is singing the national anthem tonight at the World Series?

Trend basis: Google's daily trending searches, United States, 24 October 2026 - the day's #1 query was "who is singing the national anthem tonight", as the Rays and Brewers opened the World Series in Milwaukee.

The answer first: Kylie Cantrall performs the national anthem before Game 2 of the 2026 World Series on Saturday, October 24, at American Family Field in Milwaukee. If you were asking about Game 1 on Friday night, that was four-time GRAMMY nominee Mickey Guyton. The Milwaukee Brewers won Game 1, 3-2 over the Tampa Bay Rays.

Tonight: Kylie Cantrall, Game 2

MLB announced ahead of the series that Cantrall - singer, songwriter and actress who stars as "Red" in Disney's Descendants franchise and released her EP Valley Girl Problems in 2026 - would sing the anthem ahead of Game 2 on Saturday. The ceremonial first pitch for Game 2 is Hall of Famer Paul Molitor, a key member of the Brewers' 1982 American League championship team.

Last night: Mickey Guyton, and a 3-2 Brewers win

Game 1 on Friday, October 23 opened the 122nd Fall Classic at American Family Field. Guyton performed the anthem, and the ceremonial first pitches went to Brewers legend Robin Yount and Commissioner Emeritus Bud Selig. The Brewers then withstood a Rays comeback attempt to win 3-2, taking a 1-0 series lead.

What to watch next

Game 2 gives the Rays a chance to even the series before it shifts venues. Anthem performers and ceremonial guests for later games are announced by MLB as the series progresses - the official MLB press releases are the place to confirm each night's plans.
