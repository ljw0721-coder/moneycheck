Trend basis: Google's daily trending searches, United States, 24 October 2026 — the day's #1 query was Abdul-Rakhman Yakhyaev, a fighter on today's UFC 333 card.

The answer first: UFC 333 takes place today, Saturday, October 24, 2026, at Etihad Arena in Abu Dhabi. The reason one fighter's name is topping searches in the United States is Abdul-Rakhman Yakhyaev, an undefeated light heavyweight who fights Nikita Krylov on today's card. If you came here wondering who he is, that is the short version — the rest of this post fills in the card, his record, and how to watch.

The card: two title fights on top

The main event is a featherweight title fight between champion Alexander Volkanovski and undefeated challenger Movsar Evloev. The co-main event is the third meeting between bantamweight champion Petr Yan and Merab Dvalishvili. Yakhyaev vs Krylov sits on the main card's light heavyweight slot, which is why a relatively new name is suddenly everywhere today.

Who is Yakhyaev?

Yakhyaev is 10-0 as a professional, with four knockouts, five submissions and one decision. Two finishes built his reputation: a 33-second rear-naked choke of Rafael Cerqueira at UFC Qatar in November 2025, the fastest submission by a light heavyweight in modern UFC history, and an 8-second knockout of Julius Walker in Baku on June 27, 2026, which earned Performance of the Night. He earned his UFC contract with a 30-second knockout of Alik Lorenz on Dana White's Contender Series in August 2025.

How to watch

In the United States the event streams on Paramount+, with early prelims at 10am ET, prelims at 12pm ET and the main card at 2pm ET. Krylov is the veteran test: a win over a ranked, experienced opponent would put Yakhyaev within reach of the division's top five. Note that this post was written before the event, so results were not yet final — check UFC's official results page after the card for outcomes.
