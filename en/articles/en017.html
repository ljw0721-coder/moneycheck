Answer first

Answer first: Missouri star running back Ahmad Hardy is expected to make his 2026 season debut on Saturday, October 31, at Arkansas — in a limited role, with what reports describe as a handful of carries — after missing the Tigers' first seven games while recovering from a gunshot wound suffered in May. That is the report from ESPN's Pete Thamel and CBS Sports' Matt Zenitz on Thursday, October 29; Missouri itself had not officially confirmed the debut as of Thursday evening US time, so this article says "reported" and "expected," not certain.

Key points

  • Game: No. 23 Missouri (5-2, 2-2 SEC) at Arkansas (3-4, 1-3 SEC), Saturday, October 31, 6:45 p.m. CT, SEC Network, Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium, Fayetteville.
  • Hardy was not listed on Missouri's Wednesday SEC Student-Athlete Availability Report — his first time off the "out" list this season — making him technically available.
  • Jamal Roberts remains the starter: 167 carries for 867 yards and 8 touchdowns in seven games, per Field Level Media and 247Sports.
  • In 2025 Hardy rushed for a Missouri single-season record 1,649 yards and 16 touchdowns, earning consensus All-American and SEC Newcomer of the Year honors.
  • Hardy returned to full practice on Monday, October 26; on Tuesday coach Eli Drinkwitz said there was still "no timeline" and the decision rested with Hardy, the coaches and the medical staff.

Why it's trending

Because the timeline moved fast in one week. On Sunday, October 25, Drinkwitz announced Hardy would return to practice that week. He practiced Monday and Tuesday, with Drinkwitz publicly refusing to set a timeline. On Wednesday he disappeared from the "out" list on the SEC availability report — the first concrete sign he could play. On Thursday, the Thamel and Zenitz reports of a Saturday debut turned a week of "maybe" into a specific plan, and searches for his name spiked in the United States.

What it means for readers

  • If the report holds, expect a small, managed workload — a handful of carries behind starter Jamal Roberts — not a full return in one game.
  • The real test is not Saturday's carry count but whether Hardy clears the following week at full workload, which would reshape Missouri's November SEC run.
  • Watch the official availability report before kickoff: it is the next confirmation point, and it can still change the plan.

What to watch next

The official pre-game availability update, Hardy's actual snap and carry count on Saturday night, and any word from Drinkwitz afterward on whether the medical staff signs off on a bigger role the following week.
