The answer first:

The answer first: Jacob Misiorowski is trending because he is the pitcher Milwaukee is counting on to finish the job: he starts Game 5 of the World Series at Tampa Bay on Wednesday night, with the Brewers leading the series 3-1. One night earlier, Tampa Bay refused to be swept, beating Milwaukee 4-3 in 12 innings on a pinch-hit walk-off single by Ben Williamson. A Brewers win in Game 5 gives the franchise its first championship since it began play in 1969. A Rays win sends the series back to Milwaukee for Game 6 on Friday, October 30, and, if needed, Game 7 on Saturday, October 31.

Key points

Game 4 final: Tampa Bay 4, Milwaukee 3, in 12 innings at Tropicana Field, St. Petersburg, on October 27 (US time). Milwaukee leads the series 3-1.

The winning rally: rookie Cooper Pratt's solo home run in the top of the 12th put Milwaukee ahead 3-2; in the bottom half, a Joey Ortiz throwing error put Junior Caminero aboard, and Shane Drohan allowed four consecutive singles without recording an out. Williamson's pinch-hit single won it.

Game 4 starters were Dustin May (Milwaukee, 3.0 innings, 2 runs) and Griffin Jax (Tampa Bay, 4.0 innings, 2 runs, 6 strikeouts) — the game became a bullpen duel after both left early.

Milwaukee reliever Aaron Ashby threw 2⅓ perfect innings in Game 4, extending his single-postseason scoreless relief streak to a record 18 innings, past Andrew Miller's 16 in 2016.

Misiorowski started Game 1 for Milwaukee (5 innings, 1 run) and, according to reports, threw the fastest pitch in World Series history. He sat in the bullpen through the late innings of Game 4 and never entered the game.

Game 5: Wednesday, October 28, 8:00 p.m. ET (09:00 KST, October 29) at Tropicana Field, televised on FOX in the United States. Tampa Bay's Game 5 starter had not been announced at the time of the previews.

Why it's trending

Search interest follows the decision Milwaukee manager Pat Murphy now faces, and the one he already made. In Game 4, with the Brewers three outs from a sweep, Murphy did not use Misiorowski in extra innings. Asked why, he said he did not want to burn his Game 5 starter in a one-run game on the road while leading the series 3-0 — a quote that has circulated widely since. Now the question is simpler and bigger: can the 24-year-old rookie, described in previews as the favourite for this year's National League Cy Young Award, close out the series on the road in the biggest start of his life? The fact that Misiorowski also leads the US trending list while 'j. misiorowski' and 'misiorowski' appear in Mexico's top 10 shows the same game is driving searches on both sides of the border.
