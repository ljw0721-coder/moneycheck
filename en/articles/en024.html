Gracie Abrams drops Here You Come, There You Go, announces deluxe album

Views 0

Answer first: Gracie Abrams has released a new single, "Here You Come, There You Go," alongside the announcement of "Daughter from Hell (Deluxe)," the expanded edition of her third studio album, due November 20.

Key points

  • Release: the single arrived this week (October 29-30), leading this week's New Music Friday roundups
  • Deluxe: "Daughter from Hell (Deluxe)" is scheduled for November 20
  • Credits: co-written with Amy Allen and Jim-E Stack, who also produced the track
  • Sound: a more energetic, rock-influenced pop direction than her usual understated arrangements

Why it is trending
