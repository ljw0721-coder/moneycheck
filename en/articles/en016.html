Tate McRae's 'Housewife' debuts at No. 2 on the Hot 100: a career best in a crowded chart week

Views 0

Answer first: Tate McRae's "Housewife" debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard Hot 100 — her highest-charting song as a lead artist — in an unusually crowded chart week where Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" held No. 1 for a record-extending 25th week, and new releases from Olivia Rodrigo with Chappell Roan, Zach Bryan with Lana Del Rey, and Taylor Swift filled out the rest of the Top Five.

Key points

  • "Housewife": No. 2 debut with 22.9 million streams, 12.7 million airplay audience and 4,000 sold in its first full tracking week
  • It is McRae's first release of 2026 and her second No. 1 on both the Billboard Global 200 and Global Excl. U.S. charts
  • No. 1: Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas," a record-extending 25th week on top
  • Top Five: Rodrigo×Roan "Expectations" remix at No. 3, Bryan×Del Rey "Lies" at No. 4, Swift's "Patient Zero" versions at No. 5

Why it's trending

Selection basis: the most-discussed music story of the October 29 cycle — Billboard's chart week dated October 31 (tracking October 16–22, per Luminate data cited by Billboard and Rolling Stone). It is the first time since March 23, 2025 that songs by multiple different artists debuted inside the Top Five in the same week, which drove heavy chart-watcher coverage.

Timeline

  • Oct 15–16: Rodrigo releases the "Expectations" remix with Chappell Roan; Bryan and Del Rey share "Lies"
  • Oct 16–22: tracking week; midweek predictions show four acts within a few points of No. 2
  • Midweek: "Housewife" tops Spotify's Global chart, surging in points
  • Oct 26–27: Hot 100 published — McRae takes No. 2, her best as a lead artist

Confirmed / unconfirmed

Confirmed: chart positions and consumption figures (Billboard charts via Rolling Stone and Billboard's own chart roundup), the 25-week record for "Choosin' Texas," McRae's Global 200 / Global Excl. U.S. No. 1s.

Unconfirmed: whether "Housewife" can climb to No. 1 next week — that depends on tracking data not yet collected. Any announcement of a follow-up single or album date remains unannounced.

What it means for readers

  • This was a rare week where the top of the Hot 100 was a genuine five-way race — worth listening to all five tracks rather than just the No. 1.
  • McRae's Global 200 No. 1 means the song is performing even better outside the U.S. than the Hot 100 rank suggests.

What to watch next

Next Tuesday's chart refresh (first full week for the remixes), and whether McRae announces album plans after her strongest chart week as a lead artist.
